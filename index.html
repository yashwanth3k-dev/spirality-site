Work didn't get automated. It got re-organized.

Digitized

Software replaced the paper, but the work still moves at the speed of the person holding it.

WHAT BREAKS HERE  Every department is a single-server queue. Volume goes up, the backlog goes up with it.

AI experiments

A pilot lands in one department. Meanwhile people quietly start using AI tools on their own.

WHAT BREAKS HERE  The pilot stalls on edge cases and nobody owns it. Shadow AI works — but with no authority limits and no audit trail.

AI-assisted

Everyone reaches for the AI directly. Each person gets faster, and the tools finally get used.

WHAT BREAKS HERE  Still one person per item. The gain is capped by headcount, and quality varies desk to desk.

AI-native, with Spirality

Agents carry the volume inside governed limits. People move up to supervision — they decide the exceptions and sign off. Every decision becomes memory the system reuses.

WHAT CHANGES  Nothing waits for a free pair of hands, and the exception rate keeps falling. The city around you already works this way — every quarter you wait, the gap compounds.
